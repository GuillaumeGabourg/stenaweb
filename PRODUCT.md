# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 (App Router) + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion for light animations (patterns from 21st.dev). Deploy target: Netlify (or Vercel), domain tersel.fr. Chosen by the user from a recommendation.

## Users

Owners of very small and small businesses (TPE/PME) of any trade, based in Sarthe (72), with sites already delivered in Mayenne (53), open to the neighbouring departments and to remote clients: artisans, independents, service providers, local shops. They are usually not web-savvy, have no site or an outdated one, and are evaluating who to trust with their first real website. They often arrive from a recommendation, a Google search, or a link shared by the owner.

## Product Purpose

tersel.fr is the commercial showcase of Tersel Web, a studio that designs and builds custom "sites vitrines" (showcase websites). Success = a visitor understands what they would get, sees real work, finds the price reassuring, and sends a quote request (demande de devis) or calls.

## Positioning

Agency-grade custom design at a price and speed a TPE can afford, from a local contact in Mayenne/Sarthe. Four claims together: local and close (one interlocutor, can meet in person), fast delivery, accessible price, sur-mesure (no template, fast and beautiful). Never claim the sites are "codés à la main": they are built with AI-assisted tooling, so copy stays vague about how (sur-mesure, outils modernes). The site itself is the proof: it must visibly out-class what a Wix template or a generalist agency site delivers.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare against Wix/Squarespace DIY, freelance platforms, and regional agencies. Many browse on mobile. The owner will also open the site in front of prospects as a live demo of their craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page (long one-page) + one case study page per project (`/projets/[slug]`) + legal pages (mentions légales, confidentialité — required in France).
- Quote request form (contact).
- Pricing (2026-10-15): Essentiel (non-administrable site + monthly subscription) 500 € → launch 350 €; Autonome (client-administrable, 1 year hosting + domain if available included) 700 € → launch 500 €; Sur-mesure on quote. Monthly subscription amount still to be set.
- Brand speaks as the studio "Tersel Web" (nous), not as a named individual.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tersel Web, domain tersel.fr. No existing logo — a typographic wordmark is acceptable.
- Voice: French, direct, warm, jargon-free for non-technical owners.
- Visual direction (revised 2026-10-15): the user rejected the dark/neon "agence Awwwards" canon (huge type, scroll-scrubbed animation, preloader, neon green) as unreadable and impractical for artisans. Now: soft dark (warm charcoal) with a calm accent, normal readable type, practical and functional, light one-time animations (21st.dev style), nothing scroll-scrubbed.
- No reviews section until real reviews exist. Prices are not set yet: shown as small stars + "Sur devis".
- Home sections: accueil, réalisations, services, formules, FAQ, contact (no "méthode" section).

## Product Principles

1. The site is the portfolio piece: every section should demonstrate craft a prospect could buy.
2. Wow serves trust: effects must never hide the offer, price, or contact path.
3. Speak to a business owner, not a developer: outcomes (clients, calls, credibility) before tech.
4. Real proof over claims: lead with the three live sites.
5. Fast and readable on mobile — performance and clarity are part of the pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with full non-animated fallbacks; keep text legible over animated backgrounds; WCAG AA contrast.


## Copy rules

- Say "des sites" / "sites internet", never "sites vitrines".
- Based in Sarthe, sites already done in Mayenne, never imply we only work there: neighbouring departments and remote work stay open.
