# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 (App Router) + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText) + Lenis, Motion, 21st.dev/shadcn components where useful. Deploy target: Netlify (or Vercel), domain stenaweb.fr. Chosen by the user from a recommendation.

## Users

Owners of very small and small businesses (TPE/PME) of any trade, mostly in Mayenne (53) and Sarthe (72): artisans, independents, service providers, local shops. They are usually not web-savvy, have no site or an outdated one, and are evaluating who to trust with their first real website. They often arrive from a recommendation, a Google search, or a link shared by the owner.

## Product Purpose

stenaweb.fr is the commercial showcase of Stenaweb, a studio that designs and builds custom "sites vitrines" (showcase websites). Success = a visitor understands what they would get, sees real work, finds the price reassuring, and sends a quote request (demande de devis) or calls.

## Positioning

Agency-grade, hand-coded custom design at a price and speed a TPE can afford, from a local contact in Mayenne/Sarthe. Four claims together: local and close (one interlocutor, can meet in person), fast delivery, accessible price, sur-mesure (no template, fast and beautiful code). The site itself is the proof: it must visibly out-class what a Wix template or a generalist agency site delivers.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare against Wix/Squarespace DIY, freelance platforms, and regional agencies. Many browse on mobile. The owner will also open the site in front of prospects as a live demo of their craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page (long one-page) + one case study page per project (`/projets/[slug]`) + legal pages (mentions légales, confidentialité — required in France).
- Quote request form (contact).
- Pricing shown as packaged offers. **Current prices are placeholders** (user-approved fictional values) to be replaced.
- Brand speaks as the studio "Stenaweb" (nous), not as a named individual.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Stenaweb, domain stenaweb.fr. No existing logo — a typographic wordmark is acceptable.
- Voice: French, direct, warm, jargon-free for non-technical owners.
- Visual constraint volunteered by the user: dark premium with a neon accent, very animated, "agence Awwwards" feel with strong wow effects.
- Standing preference (direction round 2026-10-12): the user chose the category standard on purpose — the dark/neon creative-agency site, executed straight, no metaphor. Quality bar: basement.studio, locomotive.ca, lusion.co, dogstudio.co.

Not yet available: testimonials from the three clients, final prices, real business metrics. Placeholder testimonials/stats are allowed for the mock-up but must be clearly replaceable and not presented as verified.

## Product Principles

1. The site is the portfolio piece: every section should demonstrate craft a prospect could buy.
2. Wow serves trust: effects must never hide the offer, price, or contact path.
3. Speak to a business owner, not a developer: outcomes (clients, calls, credibility) before tech.
4. Real proof over claims: lead with the three live sites.
5. Fast on mobile despite the motion — performance is part of the pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with full non-animated fallbacks; keep text legible over animated backgrounds; WCAG AA contrast.
